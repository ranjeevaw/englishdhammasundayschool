Lesson Reflection


Date: 31 May 2026

⁩ Today we successfully conducted the Sunday school and our junior school teacher was running some errands and was absent. She has informed that she won’t be able to come next week too.
Therefore we had 2 classes and Janaki akka managed the junior class. We had full house today 18 kids present and it’s a pleasure sight as teachers to see kids enthusiastically attending our classes. We missed Evan, Raynon, Riley and Shanon and hope they’ll join next week.
Today junior school learnt සුව ජාතකය. Where a young parrot fed his blind parents one day found delicious mangoes from a far away island.
Daddy parrot recognised the mangoes were from that island and warned the son not to go there as it was dangerous. But son didn’t listen and to serve his craving went there again and a strong cyclone threw him to the sea and perished. The story emphasised the value of listening to parents/ elders.
The year 3 and above class learned about vesak festival and the story how බෝදිසත්ව in the heaven born as a human being named prince සිද්දාර්ත with utmost courage attaining buddhahood finally discovering the 4 noble truths and help many to enlighten.
There was a funny moment when it was discovered how prince siddhartha left the castle whilst රාහුල had been born and when it was asked how sad princess යශෝදරා should have felt, seth said if i faced the situation i would have selected another partner happily. This made the whole class laugh.
We also made it a point to pay gratitude for the kids and parents who cleaned the temple yesterday in the vesak day which was a great wholesome deed.
The website will be updated tonight. If you have any ideas please let us know. Any comments good or bad are welcome. In the web site contact us page you can provide anonymous comments.
Please make sure your vesak decorations are fully lighted tonight too 😊
Have a great week ahead!
තෙරුවන් සරනය්
