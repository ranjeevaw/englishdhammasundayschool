Date: 21 June 2026

Today we successfully wrapped up the semester conducting junior class and grade 4 and above class. Junior class learned about merits of unity and were given the kids an exercise where they could write their own ideas and kids enjoyed it. Grade 4 and above class was very interesting. They learned about the bodhi tree and the pali stanzas used to worship the bodi tree. Within the class kids were asked to memorise below 2 stanzas. 1. යස්ස මූලේ නිසින්නෝව
2. ⁠ඉමේ ඒතේ මහා බෝදී
It was amazing to see almost all the kids memorised at least one stanza in the session. Then happened the very interesting part of the day. The third stanza was below.
ඉන්ද නීල වන්න පත්ත
I told kids it’s a tough one to memorise and if anyone memorise it before 4pm i will give 20$. Kids were very enthusiastic and everyone accepted the challenge.
Then Jenaru came to me and said it’s a bad practice to promise money asking kids to study. I was speechless. I told him you’re correct! This incident is open for discussion; what do you all think? I thought to myself next time i would prmoise a gift, not money ☺️
Then at 4 o’clock nobody was able to memorise it. But Nem and Senitha were very close; they told the stanza with just 2 words missing. 🫡
After that we finished the class as usual with Sunday school song and meditation.
This wrap up the semester. We distributed chocolates as usual and some biscuits from yesterday’s event. Merits to all who provided that.
We will let you know when we are resuming classes. Hope you all will have a wonderful school holidays.
May triple gems bless you all!
🙏🏿🙏🏿🙏🏿

Date: 7 June 2026

⁩ We successfully conducted 2 classes today; junior class and grade 3 and above class. Our Rev. Nandarathana and I taught the both classes one period each. Before I forget I collected flower baskets kids forgot to take homr and I can give them on 21st June, when we meet in Dhamma school. It was really encouraging to see amidst long weekend kids came to dharma school. For the grade 3 and above class pupils answered the exercise questions on lesson Upatissa and Kolitha. All the kids were keen to go through the lesson and find the answers and the discussions with Rachel was interesting. Senitha who raise nice logical questions remembered the name of Assaji thero (one of the answers for an exercise question) which made me very proud of the progress of our students. Then Seth requested to tell the story of Angulimala and we went through that story. Aura already knew the story and clearly explained how Lord Buddha controlled Angulimala. Thereafter we completed the Vesak week’s discussion about the story of Prince Siddhartha until he enlighten on Vesak poya day and touch based on Sath Sathiya. For the junior class Nandarathana thero taught the Queen Maha maya’s dream. Then we had the recess. Kids enjoyed the snacks they brought from home and chocolates which was distributed. During the recess a small incident was reported between boys and it was settled after talking to every party involved. During the recess we allow kids to play in the backyard, but it was brought to our notice that some boys are climbing on top of a block of plastic barricade which is dangerous and we’ll discuss internally and we unfortunately may need to stop kids playing outside. And also to reduce the recess time from 15 minutes to 5 minutes. Please let us know your thoughts on this. After the break the junior class learned 2 more precious stories. One story about Nandiya the leader of Deers who talked to the king and liberated all the animals / creatures from being hunted for fun and food. The other story was the famous “Sama Jathakaya”; hope you all can remember that. Our Rev. after the break tried his best to teach the kids tactics how to control emotions such as anger, bullying, being bullied, annoyance etc. He asked our senior students to hold on to a heavy object in outstretched hand and keep holding it until they couldn’t do it any more as the hand was aching. So he went ahead to describe how a small annoyance feeling if kept in your head longer, how it will eventually stress your mind and body; best to get rid of that feeling asap. Then he taught the kids if somebody is talking rough words hear it from one ear and let it go out from the other ear. After the Dhamma school song and meditation kids went home on their merry ways. Next week there’s NO Sunday school. Please drop your kids to the drama excursion in Cranbourne. Details will be shared soon. Drama is starting at 3pm. Any questions, comments, suggestions feel free to discuss here or anonymously send a message through Sunday school web site (Contact Us page). May triple gems blessings be with you!! 🙏🏿🙏🏿🙏🏿
