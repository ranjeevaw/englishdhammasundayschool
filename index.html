Welcome

Welcome to English Dhamma Sunday School web site.

May Triple Gems Bless You 👋

We are the English Dhamma Sunday School, a proud initiative of the English Dhamma Organisation. Our mission is to disseminate the teachings of Lord Buddha to Buddhist children in Australia through the English language, helping them develop strong moral values, mindfulness, compassion, and respect within a modern multicultural environment.

Our school follows the Sri Lankan Buddhist Dhamma School curriculum, carefully adapted and delivered in English to make learning meaningful and accessible for children growing up in Australia.

Currently, we conduct classes across three learning groups:

* Junior Class – Prep, Grade 1, and Grade 2 students
* Year 3 Class
* Year 4 and Above Class

We warmly welcome students up to 15 years of age.

At English Dhamma Sunday School, we strongly value the partnership between teachers and parents. We maintain close communication with families and strive to shape our teaching methods to best support the aspirations and expectations parents have for their children’s spiritual and personal development.

We invite you and your family to join us in nurturing the next generation with Buddhist values, kindness, discipline, and wisdom in a warm and supportive community environment.
